A Day I Can Never Forget

Hi everyone,good morning!One of the most unforgettable and difficult incidents in my life happened when I was about to start my 12th standard. One night, I went to a function with my father,younger brother and sister. We were travelling back home in our XL bike. Suddenly, a vehicle coming from the opposite direction hit us directly. The people in that vehicle did not even stop to check on us. We all fell on the road, and I fell a little further in front because of the speed of the accident.After that, my father somehow got us up and took us home. We were all injured on our hands, legs, and other parts of our body. My mother had to take care of all of us, and it was a very difficult time for our whole family.I also had a fracture in my leg and a knee injury. Even after treatment, my leg did not completely recover. Sometimes, even a small hit to my leg can still cause pain.That accident was a very difficult experience for me and my family. It affected me physically and emotionally,and i still remember that day clearly.its happened a few years ago,but it has affected me even now.the accident will always remain one of the most unforgettable incidents of my life.
